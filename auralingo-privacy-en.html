AuraLingo Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 11, 2026

AuraLingo is developed by Yaogdu ("we"). We respect your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Collect

  • No account or login is required. We do not directly collect personal identifiers.
  • Learning data (e.g., progress, study history, favorites, settings) is stored locally on your device.
  • If you enable iCloud Sync, the app syncs your learning data via Apple CloudKit to your iCloud account. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

How We Use Information

  • To provide core features such as wordbook progress, insights, and sync.
  • To keep your data consistent across devices when iCloud Sync is enabled.

In-App Purchases

AuraLingo offers a one-time Pro purchase. Payments are processed by Apple via the App Store. We do not receive your payment information. Apple may process related data under its own privacy policy: Apple Privacy Policy.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Data may be processed by Apple as part of iCloud or App Store services.

Data Retention

Your learning data is stored locally and, if enabled, in your iCloud account. You can remove local data by uninstalling the app. You can manage iCloud data from your Apple ID settings.

Children's Privacy

AuraLingo does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data stored in the app, but no method of storage is 100% secure.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, contact yaogdu@gmail.com.
