AuraShow Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 21, 2026

AuraShow is developed by Yaogdu ("we"). We value your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Collect

  • The app does not require registration or login and does not directly collect personal data.
  • Text you enter is used only for on-device display and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Display settings (such as font size, colors, and scroll speed) are stored locally on your device.

In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time Pro purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple via the App Store and we do not receive your payment information. Apple may process related data under its own privacy policy: Apple Privacy Policy.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties unless required by law.

Data Retention

The app stores settings locally on your device. You can remove local data by uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect local data, but no method is 100% secure.

Contact Us

If you have questions, contact yaogdu@gmail.com.
