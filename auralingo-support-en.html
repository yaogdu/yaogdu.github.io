AuraLingo Support

If you need help or have questions, contact yaogdu@gmail.com.

Common Questions

  • Restore Purchase: Settings → Pro Membership → Restore.
  • iCloud Sync: Make sure iCloud is enabled for AuraLingo in Settings.
  • Wordbook Progress: Progress updates after study sessions and syncs if iCloud is on.

Troubleshooting

  • Update to the latest app version.
  • Toggle iCloud Sync off and on again if sync appears stuck.
  • If issues persist, email support with your device model and iOS version.
